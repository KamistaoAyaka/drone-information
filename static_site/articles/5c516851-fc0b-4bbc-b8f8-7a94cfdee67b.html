Less Than $400 Million Needed to Modify VC-25B Bridge Aircraft, USAF Estimates

📰 Aviation Today 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

The United States Air Force (USAF) has estimated that modifying the VC-25B bridge aircraft, which will serve as an interim presidential transport, will require less than $400 million. This cost projection covers necessary upgrades and alterations to ensure the aircraft meets operational and security standards before the next-generation Air Force One fleet is fully operational. The VC-25B, derived from a Boeing 747-8 platform, is intended to bridge the gap between the current aging VC-25A aircraft and the future presidential airlift capability. The USAF’s estimate reflects a focused effort to minimize expenses while maintaining high reliability and safety for presidential missions. The modification process includes integrating advanced communication systems, defensive countermeasures, and interior configurations tailored for executive use. This relatively modest budget, compared to larger defense programs, underscores the USAF’s strategy to efficiently manage resources during the transition period. The project remains on track, with the modified aircraft expected to enter service in the coming years, ensuring uninterrupted presidential airlift capability.
