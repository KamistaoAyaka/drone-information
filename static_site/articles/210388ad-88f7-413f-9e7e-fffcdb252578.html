DroneShield宣布获得美国联合跨机构特遣部队401合同

文章摘要

DroneShield has announced a contract with the US Joint Interagency Task Force 401, a specialized unit focused on counter-drone operations. The agreement involves the supply of DroneShield’s counter-unmanned aerial systems (C-UAS) technology, including detection and mitigation equipment. This contract underscores the growing demand for drone defense solutions in military and interagency settings. Key details include the deployment of DroneShield’s products to enhance operational security against unauthorized drone threats. The company expects the contract to contribute to its revenue and strengthen its presence in the US defense market. No specific financial terms were disclosed.
