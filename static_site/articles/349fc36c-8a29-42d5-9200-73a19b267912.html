SkyfireAI has secured $11 million in funding to advance its autonomous drone coordination technology. The investment will support the development of software enabling multiple drones to operate collaboratively without direct human control, focusing on applications in surveillance, inspection, and emergency response. Key features include real-time data sharing, obstacle avoidance, and mission planning across drone swarms. The funding round was led by prominent venture capital firms, reflecting growing interest in autonomous systems for commercial and public safety uses. SkyfireAI aims to enhance efficiency and reduce costs in industries such as agriculture, logistics, and infrastructure monitoring. The company plans to deploy its technology for large-scale operations, emphasizing safety and regulatory compliance.
