星舰（Starship）第12次飞行测试更接近发射，助推器和飞船完成静态点火测试

📰 Drone DJ 📅 2026-05-18 🌍 国外

文章摘要

SpaceX's Starship program advanced toward its next flight with successful static fire tests of both the Super Heavy booster and the Starship upper stage. The booster ignited all 33 Raptor engines at the launch site, while the ship conducted a separate static fire. These tests validate key systems ahead of Flight 12, which aims to demonstrate orbital capabilities and reusability. The milestone brings the vehicle closer to launch, pending regulatory approval and final checks.
