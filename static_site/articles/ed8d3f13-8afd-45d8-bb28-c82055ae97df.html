文章摘要

Skydio has commenced a $3.5 billion expansion of its drone manufacturing operations in the United States. The initiative aims to scale up production capacity to meet growing demand from both commercial and defense sectors. Key aspects include building new facilities, increasing workforce, and enhancing supply chain resilience. The expansion is partly driven by federal contracts and policies favoring domestic drone manufacturing over foreign competitors, particularly Chinese-made drones. Skydio’s move is expected to strengthen U.S. leadership in drone technology and create thousands of jobs.
