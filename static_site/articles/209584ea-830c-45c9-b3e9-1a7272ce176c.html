U.S. Air Force Expands X10D EOD Program With Multi-Million Dollar Follow-On Award

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

The U.S. Air Force has significantly expanded its X10D Explosive Ordnance Disposal (EOD) program by issuing a multi-million dollar follow-on contract award. This new funding, valued in the millions, aims to enhance the capabilities of the X10D system, which is designed to improve the safety and efficiency of bomb disposal operations. The award will support the procurement of additional equipment, advanced training for personnel, and further research and development to address emerging threats. By investing in this program, the Air Force seeks to modernize its EOD capabilities, ensuring that technicians have access to state-of-the-art tools for detecting and neutralizing explosive hazards. The expansion reflects the military’s ongoing commitment to protecting both service members and civilians in high-risk environments. This follow-on contract builds on previous successes of the X10D program, which has demonstrated effectiveness in field operations. Overall, the initiative underscores the Air Force’s strategic focus on leveraging technology to mitigate risks associated with explosive threats.
