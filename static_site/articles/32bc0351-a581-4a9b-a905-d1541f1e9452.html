Eurosatory 2026：Milrem公司展示集成EOS反无人机武器站（C-UAS weapon station）的THeMIS无人地面车辆（UGV）

📰 Unmanned Airspace 📅 2026-06-16 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 Milrem, EOS

文章摘要

At Eurosatory 2026, Milrem Robotics showcased its THeMIS unmanned ground vehicle (UGV) integrated with an EOS counter-unmanned aircraft system (C-UAS) weapon station. The system combines Milrem’s tracked UGV platform with EOS’s remote weapon station, designed to detect, track, and neutralize hostile drones. The integration enhances autonomous ground capabilities for anti-drone operations, providing a mobile and scalable solution for force protection. Key features include modular design, remote operation, and compatibility with various sensors and effectors. The demonstration highlights growing military interest in unmanned systems for countering evolving aerial threats.
