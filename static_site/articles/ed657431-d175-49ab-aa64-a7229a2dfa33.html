Why SpaceX thinks it's worth nearly $2 trillion

文章摘要

SpaceX believes it is worth nearly $2 trillion due to its dominant position in the space industry and its ambitious long-term vision. The company’s valuation is driven by its revolutionary reusable rocket technology, which drastically reduces launch costs and enables frequent missions. Its Starlink satellite internet service, already generating significant revenue with millions of subscribers, is expected to become a major profit center, potentially dominating global broadband markets. Additionally, SpaceX’s Starship program aims to enable interplanetary travel, including crewed missions to Mars, opening up unprecedented commercial opportunities in space exploration, resource mining, and colonization. The company also holds lucrative contracts with NASA and the U.S. Department of Defense, further solidifying its financial stability. By combining near-term profitability from Starlink and launch services with long-term potential in space settlement, SpaceX projects a future where it controls critical infrastructure in orbit and beyond, justifying its trillion-dollar valuation.
