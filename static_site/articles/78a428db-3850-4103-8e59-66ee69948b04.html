Notch Launches MAGIC Series to Replace Omni Antennas and More Than Double Wireless Range

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

Notch has officially launched its new MAGIC Series, designed to replace its previous Omni Antennas while significantly enhancing wireless performance. The MAGIC Series introduces advanced antenna technology that more than doubles the wireless range compared to the earlier Omni models. This improvement is achieved through innovative design and engineering, offering users greater connectivity and reliability in various applications, including IoT, smart home systems, and industrial wireless networks. The series maintains compatibility with existing Notch devices, ensuring a seamless upgrade path for current users. By focusing on extended range and robust signal strength, the MAGIC Series aims to address common limitations in wireless communication, such as signal degradation over distance and interference. Notch emphasizes that the new antennas are optimized for both indoor and outdoor environments, providing consistent performance across diverse settings. With this launch, Notch reinforces its commitment to delivering cutting-edge wireless solutions that meet evolving market demands for longer-range, more dependable connectivity.
