The Supersonic Race: Who Will Win the Skies?

📰 Aviation Today 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

The supersonic aviation race has reignited as multiple companies and nations compete to develop the next generation of commercial aircraft capable of exceeding the speed of sound. Key players include Boom Supersonic, with its Overture jet aiming for Mach 1.7, and NASA’s X-59 QueSST, focused on reducing sonic booms to enable overland flights. Meanwhile, startups like Hermeus and Spike Aerospace are pursuing hypersonic and supersonic designs, while established aerospace giants such as Lockheed Martin and Boeing explore advanced concepts. The competition centers on overcoming challenges like fuel efficiency, noise regulations, and economic viability. While Boom Supersonic leads in pre-orders and testing, regulatory hurdles and environmental concerns remain significant. The outcome of this race will determine whether supersonic travel returns commercially, potentially reducing transatlantic flight times by half. However, no clear winner has emerged, as technological breakthroughs and market adoption will ultimately decide who claims the skies.
