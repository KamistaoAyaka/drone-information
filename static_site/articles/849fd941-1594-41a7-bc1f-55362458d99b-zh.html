Colorado Drone Chargers Unveils the Ultimate Power Solution for the Skydio X10: Featuring New OMNI FLUX Technology Press Colorado Drone Chargers (CDC), the leader in high-performance commercial drone battery charging systems, is proud to announce the launch of the Skydio X10 Portable Rapid Charging System. Meticulously engineered for the industry-leading Skydio X10 drone, this new system is designed to provide drone operators with unprecedented battery charging capabilities, making it the most efficient charging solution available for the X10. As drone professionals increasingly adopt the Skydio X10 for critical missions, the need for minimized downtime is imperative for mission success. The CDC Skydio X10 system addresses this by charging four batteries simultaneously in an average of just one hour, effectively providing more flight time with less batteries. The Power of OMNI FLUXAt the heart of this new charging system is CDCs latest breakthrough in battery management: the OMNI FLUX board. Specifically designed for CDC this new, proprietary technology optimizes charging rates by managing and balancing power across all channels. Key technical features of the OMNI FLUX technology include: Intelligent Power Management: Master board balancing ensures maximum efficiency whether charging one pack or several. Generator Optimized: Universal AC input with Power Factor Correction (PFC) allows the system to pull clean power even from dirty generator or inverter sources often found in remote environments. Advanced Safety: CPU-controlled voltage and current management ensures batteries are charged safely, protecting your battery investment. Versatile Charging: Features four integrated USB portstwo USB-A and two USB-Cto charge corded accessories alongside your batteries. Built for the Rugged Professional True to the Colorado Drone Chargers standard, the Skydio X10 charging system is securely housed in a durable hard case designed for the most demanding applications.
