SkyfireAI 为自主无人机协调筹集 1100 万美元

📰 DroneDJ 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 蜂群/集群 🏢 SkyfireAI

文章摘要

SkyfireAI has secured $11 million in funding to advance its autonomous drone coordination technology. The investment will support the development of a system enabling multiple drones to operate collaboratively without direct human control, focusing on applications in surveillance, inspection, and emergency response. Key features include real-time data sharing, obstacle avoidance, and mission planning among drone swarms. The funding round was led by prominent venture capital firms, with participation from existing investors. SkyfireAI aims to enhance efficiency and safety in industries such as agriculture, logistics, and public safety by reducing reliance on individual drone operators. The company plans to use the capital to expand its engineering team, accelerate product deployment, and pursue regulatory approvals for commercial use. This funding highlights growing interest in autonomous drone systems for complex, coordinated tasks.
