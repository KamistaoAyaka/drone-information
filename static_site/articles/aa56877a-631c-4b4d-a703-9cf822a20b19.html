Air Force Plans $1.7 Billion Retrofit Of APG-85 Radar On 181 F-35As In Lot 17 And Prior

📰 Aviation Today 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

The U.S. Air Force has announced plans to allocate $1.7 billion for a comprehensive retrofit program aimed at upgrading the APG-85 radar system on 181 F-35A Lightning II aircraft. This initiative specifically targets jets produced in Lot 17 and all prior production lots. The APG-85 radar represents a significant technological advancement over earlier systems, offering enhanced detection range, improved electronic warfare capabilities, and superior target tracking performance. The retrofit program underscores the Air Force's commitment to maintaining air superiority by ensuring that older F-35A variants remain operationally relevant against evolving threats. By standardizing the radar configuration across a substantial portion of the fleet, the service aims to reduce logistical complexity and improve combat effectiveness. The $1.7 billion investment covers procurement, installation, testing, and integration of the new radar systems, with work expected to be carried out over several years. This upgrade is part of broader efforts to modernize the F-35 fleet and extend the service life of early production aircraft.
