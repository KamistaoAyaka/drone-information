摩托罗拉解决方案公司（Motorola Solutions）将收购反无人机系统行业领导者D-Fend Solutions（D-Fend解决方案公司）

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 特种无人机 🏢 Motorola

文章摘要

Motorola Solutions has announced its acquisition of D-Fend Solutions, a leading provider of counter-drone technology. D-Fend specializes in radio frequency-based, non-kinetic drone mitigation systems designed to detect, track, and safely neutralize unauthorized drones without causing physical damage. The acquisition aims to enhance Motorola Solutions' existing security and public safety portfolio by integrating advanced drone detection and response capabilities. Key details include D-Fend’s proven technology used by government and critical infrastructure clients, and the deal’s focus on addressing the growing threat of rogue drones. The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2024, subject to regulatory approvals. Financial terms were not disclosed.
