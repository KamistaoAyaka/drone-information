英文原标题：Eurosatory 2025 – Rheinmetall launches Skyspotter C-UAS system to protect critical infrastructure

📰 Unmanned Airspace 📅 2026-06-17 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 Rheinmetall

Rheinmetall is presenting the Skyspotter counter-UAS system at this year’s Eurosatory 2026 – a specialised solution for the protection of critical infrastructure.

“The Skyspotter was developed as a multi-sensor early warning system,” says the company in a press release. “Its primary task is to detect potential airborne threats well in advance, identify them precisely and track them seamlessly. By intelligently combining various sensor technologies, the system provides security forces with the necessary real-time information to make decisions and respond flexibly to threats in the sensitive airspace around airports.”
