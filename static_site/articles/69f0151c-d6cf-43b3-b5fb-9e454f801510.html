“超过30台Robin Radar IRIS无人机探测器（Robin Radar IRIS drone detectors）被用于保护FIFA世界杯（FIFA World Cup）场馆”

📰 Unmanned Airspace 📅 2026-06-11 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 Robin Radar

文章摘要

Over 30 Robin Radar IRIS drone detectors are being deployed to secure FIFA World Cup venues. These systems are designed to detect and track unauthorized drones, providing critical protection against potential aerial threats during the tournament. The IRIS detectors utilize advanced radar technology to identify small drones at long ranges, enabling security teams to respond swiftly. This deployment underscores the growing importance of counter-drone measures in safeguarding large-scale international events.
