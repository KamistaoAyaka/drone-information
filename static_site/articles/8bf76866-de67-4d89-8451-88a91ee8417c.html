📰 Unmanned Airspace 📅 2026-06-18 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 CERBAIR, Delair

CERBAIR and Delair have signed a long-term strategic partnership, “the first major industrial merger in France between a drone manufacturer and an anti-drone manufacturer, to build a sovereign next-generation offer in anti-drone warfare and airborne electronic warfare,” according to a CERBAIR Linkedin post.

The cooperative agreement will bring together CERBAIR’s detection, identification and electromagnetic jamming technology with DELAIR’s ASPIK interceptor drone equipped with an explosive charge.
