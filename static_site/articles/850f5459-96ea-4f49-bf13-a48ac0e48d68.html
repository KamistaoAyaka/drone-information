法国反无人机系统（C-UAS）公司CERBAIR“被Latour Capital收购”

📰 Unmanned Airspace 📅 2026-06-16 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 CERBAIR, Latour Capital

文章摘要

French C-UAS company CERBAIR has been acquired by Latour Capital. The acquisition aims to support CERBAIR's growth in the defense and security sectors, particularly in counter-drone technology. CERBAIR specializes in detecting, identifying, and neutralizing unauthorized drones, offering solutions for military, governmental, and industrial clients. Latour Capital, a private equity firm, will provide resources to accelerate CERBAIR's commercial development and expand its market presence. The deal underscores increasing demand for anti-drone systems amid rising drone threats. Financial terms were not disclosed.
