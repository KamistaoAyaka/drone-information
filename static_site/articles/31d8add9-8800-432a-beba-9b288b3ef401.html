Hermeus公司的Quarterhorse Mk 2.1飞机在首次无人飞行中实现超音速

📰 Aviation Today 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 固定翼 🏢 Hermeus

文章摘要

Hermeus’ Quarterhorse Mk 2.1 has become the first unmanned aircraft to achieve supersonic flight, reaching Mach 1.1 during a test over the Atlantic Ocean. The flight, which took place from a launch site in Florida, marks a significant milestone for the company’s hypersonic development program. The Mk 2.1 is a subscale prototype designed to validate key technologies, including the Pratt & Whitney F100 engine and thermal management systems. The aircraft accelerated through the sound barrier without the need for an afterburner, demonstrating efficient supersonic cruise capability. This achievement paves the way for future iterations, including the larger Quarterhorse Mk 3, which aims to reach Mach 4. The test also provides critical data for Hermeus’ long-term goal of developing a reusable hypersonic aircraft for commercial and defense applications.
