New DroneLight from Esh-Tech Delivers Hard-Kill Laser Defense to the Tactical Edge, Neutralizing Drones in 1-2 Seconds

文章摘要

Esh-Tech has introduced DroneLight, a new hard-kill laser defense system designed for tactical edge operations. The system neutralizes drones within 1-2 seconds, offering rapid response against unmanned aerial threats. DroneLight is compact and portable, enabling deployment in field environments where traditional air defense systems are impractical. It uses a high-energy laser to physically destroy drones, providing a direct kinetic effect rather than jamming or spoofing. The system is intended for military and security forces requiring immediate, on-site counter-drone capabilities. Key features include fast engagement time, precision targeting, and low collateral risk. Esh-Tech positions DroneLight as a solution for protecting troops, convoys, and fixed installations from small drone swarms and single threats. The product represents a shift toward directed-energy weapons at the tactical level, emphasizing speed and lethality.
