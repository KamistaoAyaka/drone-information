摩托罗拉解决方案公司（Motorola Solutions）将收购反无人机系统行业领导者D-Fend Solutions（D-Fend Solutions）

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 Motorola Solutions

文章摘要

Motorola Solutions has announced its acquisition of D-Fend Solutions, a leading provider of counter-drone technology. D-Fend specializes in radio frequency-based, non-kinetic drone detection and mitigation systems, designed to address unauthorized drone activity in sensitive areas such as airports, critical infrastructure, and public events. The acquisition aims to enhance Motorola Solutions’ existing security and public safety portfolio by integrating advanced drone defense capabilities. Key terms of the deal, including financial details, were not disclosed. The transaction is expected to close in the first quarter of 2025, subject to regulatory approvals. This move reflects the growing demand for comprehensive security solutions against evolving drone threats.
