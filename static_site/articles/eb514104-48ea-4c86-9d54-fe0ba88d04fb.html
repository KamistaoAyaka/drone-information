DroneShield宣布获得美国联合跨机构特遣部队401合同

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 DroneShield

文章摘要

DroneShield has announced a contract with the US Joint Interagency Task Force 401 for the supply of counter-drone technology. The contract involves the provision of DroneShield’s electronic warfare and detection systems to enhance security operations against unauthorized drone threats. Key details include the deployment of the company’s DroneSentry and DroneGun products, which are designed for detection, tracking, and neutralization of drones. The agreement underscores the growing demand for counter-unmanned aerial system (C-UAS) solutions within US defense and security agencies. Financial terms of the contract were not disclosed, but it is expected to contribute to DroneShield’s revenue stream and strengthen its position in the C-UAS market. The contract reflects ongoing collaboration between DroneShield and US government entities to address evolving drone-based threats.
