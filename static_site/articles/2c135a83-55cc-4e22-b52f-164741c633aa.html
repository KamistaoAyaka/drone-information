DroneShield Announces US Joint Interagency Task Force 401 Contract

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外

文章摘要

DroneShield has announced a contract with the US Joint Interagency Task Force 401, a military unit focused on counter-narcotics and counter-threat operations. The contract involves the supply of DroneShield’s counter-drone technology, including its DroneSentry-X systems and related equipment, to enhance the task force’s capability to detect and mitigate unauthorized drone activity. This agreement underscores DroneShield’s growing presence in the US defense market and its role in providing advanced electronic warfare solutions. The contract value was not disclosed, but it marks a significant operational deployment for the company’s technology in a joint interagency environment.
