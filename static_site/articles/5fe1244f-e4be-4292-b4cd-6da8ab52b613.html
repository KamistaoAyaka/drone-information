FAA Announces Strict Airspace Restrictions for FIFA World Cup 2026

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

The Federal Aviation Administration (FAA) has officially announced the implementation of strict airspace restrictions for the FIFA World Cup 2026, which will be co-hosted by the United States, Canada, and Mexico. These measures are designed to ensure the safety and security of the event, which is expected to draw millions of spectators and participants. The restrictions will apply to all aircraft, including drones, within designated zones around stadiums and key venues during match days and related activities. Specific no-fly zones and temporary flight restrictions (TFRs) will be enforced, with exceptions only for authorized aircraft such as emergency services, military, and approved media operations. Violators may face severe penalties, including interception by law enforcement, civil fines, and criminal charges. The FAA emphasizes that these rules are crucial to prevent potential threats and disruptions, aligning with international security protocols. Detailed maps and schedules of the restricted areas will be released closer to the tournament dates, and pilots are urged to review all notices to airmen (NOTAMs) for compliance.
