Metis将为KONGSBERG提供用于C-UAS SAN项目的被动射频探测器（passive RF detectors）

📰 Unmanned Airspace 📅 2026-06-16 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 Metis, KONGSBERG

文章摘要

Metis will supply passive RF detectors to KONGSBERG for the C-UAS SAN project. These detectors are designed to identify and track unmanned aerial systems (UAS) by analyzing their radio frequency emissions without actively transmitting signals. The technology enhances situational awareness and counter-drone capabilities by detecting, classifying, and locating potential threats. The collaboration aims to integrate Metis’s passive RF detection systems into KONGSBERG’s broader counter-unmanned aircraft system (C-UAS) solutions, supporting defense and security operations. The project focuses on improving detection accuracy and reducing false alarms in complex environments.
