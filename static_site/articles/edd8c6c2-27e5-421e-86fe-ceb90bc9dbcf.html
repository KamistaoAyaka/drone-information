The article discusses a consultation on certification requirements for Unmanned Aircraft Systems (UAS) Traffic Management (UTM). It outlines the need for a standardized framework to ensure safe integration of drones into airspace. Key points include proposed certification criteria for UTM service providers, focusing on operational safety, data security, and interoperability with existing air traffic control systems. The consultation seeks stakeholder feedback on technical standards, performance metrics, and regulatory compliance to support scalable drone operations. It emphasizes the importance of establishing clear guidelines for UTM system reliability and accountability to mitigate risks such as collisions and unauthorized flights. The process aims to harmonize international practices and facilitate commercial drone use while maintaining public safety.
