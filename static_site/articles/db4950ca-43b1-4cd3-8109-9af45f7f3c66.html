Drone Makers ACSL and Draganfly Partner to Bring NDAA-Compliant Japanese Drones to Canadian Market; Signs Exclusive Distributor Agreement and Launches Technology Integration

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

ACSL, a Japanese drone manufacturer, and Draganfly, a Canadian drone solutions provider, have announced a strategic partnership. They signed an exclusive distributor agreement that allows Draganfly to bring ACSL’s NDAA-compliant drones to the Canadian market. The partnership also includes technology integration between the two companies. This collaboration aims to offer secure, reliable drone solutions that meet the National Defense Authorization Act (NDAA) compliance standards, addressing growing demand for trusted, non-Chinese drone technology in North America. The agreement positions Draganfly as the exclusive distributor of ACSL drones in Canada, enhancing both companies' market reach and technological capabilities.
