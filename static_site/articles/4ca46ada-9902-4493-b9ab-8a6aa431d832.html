Wing和沃尔玛（Walmart）将无人机配送业务扩展至美国七个新城市

英文原标题：Wing and Walmart bring drone delivery operations to seven new US cities

📰 Unmanned Airspace 📅 2026-06-18 🌍 国外 🛸 多旋翼 🏢 Wing, Walmart

Wing and Walmart have announced seven new major metro drone delivery area: Memphis, New Orleans, Philadelphia, Phoenix, San Diego, San Francisco Bay Area and Salt Lake City.

“This planned expansion into more regions will bring Wing and Walmart’s total service footprint to nearly 20 U.S. markets across the country, leading the industry and advancing our work to build a nationwide drone delivery network,” said the company in a news release.

“Our work with Walmart has shown that drone delivery isn’t just a novelty, it’s a service many customers count on multiple times per week,” said Heather Rivera, Wing’s Chief Business Officer. “We’re already working with many communities in the seven new markets, as we accelerate our progress to bring ultra-fast delivery to 40 million residents throughout the U.S.”

Residents in these new markets will soon join residents with access to this service in Dallas-Fort Worth, Metro Atlanta, and Greater Houston. This latest phase builds upon the company’s previous announcements in Orlando, Tampa, Charlotte, St. Louis, Cincinnati, Los Angeles and Miami.
