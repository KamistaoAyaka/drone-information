Hermeus的Quarterhorse Mk 2.1飞机在首次无人飞行中实现超音速

📰 Aviation Today 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外

文章摘要

Hermeus’ Quarterhorse Mk 2.1 has become the first unmanned aircraft to achieve supersonic flight. The milestone flight demonstrated the vehicle’s ability to break the sound barrier, marking a significant step in the development of high-speed, reusable unmanned aerial systems. The aircraft is part of Hermeus’ broader effort to create a hypersonic commercial and military aircraft. Key details include the successful transition through Mach 1 and the validation of the aircraft’s thermal and propulsion systems under supersonic conditions. This achievement positions Hermeus as a leader in unmanned supersonic flight technology.
