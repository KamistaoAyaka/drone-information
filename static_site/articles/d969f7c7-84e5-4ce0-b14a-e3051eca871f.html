USAF To Meet With Companies On Support Equipment For B-52 CERP

📰 Aviation Today 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

The United States Air Force (USAF) is scheduled to hold meetings with industry partners to discuss support equipment requirements for the B-52 Commercial Engine Replacement Program (CERP). This initiative aims to modernize the aging B-52 Stratofortress fleet by replacing its current engines with more efficient and reliable commercial alternatives. The meetings will focus on identifying and developing necessary ground support equipment, including maintenance tools, test systems, and logistical infrastructure, to ensure seamless integration and sustainment of the new engines. The USAF seeks to leverage private sector expertise to enhance operational readiness, reduce lifecycle costs, and extend the bomber's service life into the 2050s. By engaging with companies early in the process, the Air Force aims to streamline procurement and avoid potential delays. This collaborative effort underscores the military's commitment to maintaining strategic deterrence capabilities while adapting to evolving technological and budgetary demands.
