法国反无人机系统（C-UAS）公司CERBAIR“被Latour Capital收购”

📰 Unmanned Airspace 📅 2026-06-16 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 CERBAIR, Latour Capital

文章摘要

French counter-drone systems company CERBAIR has been acquired by investment firm Latour Capital. CERBAIR specializes in developing and manufacturing anti-drone solutions, including detection and jamming systems for protecting sensitive sites against unauthorized UAVs. The acquisition aims to accelerate CERBAIR's growth, expand its commercial reach, and strengthen its position in the European and international counter-UAS market. Latour Capital's investment will provide financial resources and strategic support to enhance CERBAIR's product development and operational capabilities. The deal reflects growing demand for counter-drone technologies amid increasing drone threats to critical infrastructure, airports, and public events.
