Artemis 2's launch in photos, closer than any human can get

📰 Drone DJ 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

The Artemis 2 mission, captured in a series of stunning photographs, marks a historic milestone as it brings humans closer to the Moon than any crewed mission in over 50 years. These images document the launch and journey of NASA's Orion spacecraft, carrying a crew of four astronauts on a trajectory that will take them within approximately 130 kilometers of the lunar surface—closer than any human has approached since the Apollo era. The photos highlight key moments, including the powerful liftoff from Kennedy Space Center, the separation of rocket stages, and the spacecraft's transit through Earth's orbit. Unlike the Apollo missions, Artemis 2 does not land on the Moon but instead performs a lunar flyby, testing critical systems for future deep-space exploration. The visual record serves as both a technical documentation and an inspirational portrayal of human ambition, showcasing the spacecraft against the backdrop of Earth and the Moon. These images provide an unprecedented, up-close perspective of humanity's return to lunar vicinity, paving the way for Artemis 3's planned landing.
