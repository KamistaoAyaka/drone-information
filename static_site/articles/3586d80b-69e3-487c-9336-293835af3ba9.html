Motorola Solutions to Acquire D-Fend Solutions, an Industry Leader in Counter-Drone Systems

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外

文章摘要

Motorola Solutions has announced its acquisition of D-Fend Solutions, a leading provider of counter-drone technology. D-Fend specializes in radio frequency-based, non-kinetic drone mitigation systems designed to detect, track, and safely neutralize unauthorized drones without causing physical destruction. The acquisition aims to enhance Motorola Solutions’ existing safety and security portfolio, particularly for critical infrastructure, government, and public safety clients facing growing threats from rogue drones. Key details include D-Fend’s proprietary software-defined radio technology and its proven deployment in sensitive environments such as airports and correctional facilities. The deal underscores the increasing demand for advanced counter-unmanned aircraft systems (C-UAS) in both commercial and defense sectors. Financial terms of the transaction were not disclosed. The acquisition is expected to close in the first quarter of 2024, subject to regulatory approvals.
