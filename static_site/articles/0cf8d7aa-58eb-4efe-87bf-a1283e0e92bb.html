Fisker went bankrupt and owners built open source car company from the ashes

📰 Drone DJ 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

Following the bankruptcy of Fisker Automotive, a group of dedicated vehicle owners and enthusiasts transformed the company's collapse into an opportunity for innovation. Rather than abandoning the technology and designs, they established an open-source car company built from the remnants of Fisker’s assets. This new venture aimed to democratize automotive development by releasing blueprints, software, and engineering data to the public. The community-driven model allowed former owners, engineers, and developers to collaboratively improve and maintain the vehicles, ensuring that Fisker’s electric car technology would not be lost. By embracing open-source principles, the group sought to lower barriers to entry in the automotive industry, foster transparency, and create a sustainable ecosystem for electric vehicle innovation. This grassroots effort demonstrated how a corporate failure could be repurposed into a collaborative, community-owned enterprise, offering a unique alternative to traditional car manufacturing.
