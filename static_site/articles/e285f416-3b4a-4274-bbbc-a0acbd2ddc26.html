NATS、DroneCloud与Network Rail完成试验，解锁关键基础设施周边的无人机运行能力

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 DroneCloud

文章摘要

NATS, DroneCloud, and Network Rail have successfully completed a trial aimed at enabling safe drone operations near critical infrastructure, such as railways. The trial tested a system integrating drone traffic management with existing air traffic control to ensure drones can operate without interfering with manned aircraft or sensitive sites. Key outcomes include demonstrating real-time data sharing between drone operators and air traffic services, and validating procedures for drone flights in controlled airspace. This collaboration highlights progress toward routine drone use for infrastructure inspection and maintenance, potentially improving efficiency and safety while reducing costs and disruption. The trial represents a step forward in integrating unmanned aircraft into national airspace systems.
