Another African country targets fossil-free electric transit by 2030

📰 Drone DJ 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

Another African country has announced an ambitious plan to transition its public transportation system to fully fossil-free electric transit by the year 2030. This initiative aligns with a growing trend across the continent to adopt sustainable energy solutions and reduce carbon emissions. The country aims to replace traditional diesel and petrol-powered buses, taxis, and other forms of public transport with electric vehicles (EVs), supported by the development of necessary charging infrastructure and renewable energy sources. The move is expected to significantly lower air pollution in urban areas, decrease dependence on imported fossil fuels, and create new economic opportunities in the green technology sector. While challenges such as high initial costs, grid reliability, and maintenance capacity remain, the government is exploring partnerships with international investors and technology providers to accelerate the transition. This commitment positions the nation as a leader in Africa’s clean energy movement, following similar pledges by other countries like Kenya, Rwanda, and Ethiopia to electrify their transport systems.
