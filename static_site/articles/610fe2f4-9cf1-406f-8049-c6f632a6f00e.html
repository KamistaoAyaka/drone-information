Hermeus公司的Quarterhorse Mk 2.1飞机在首次无人飞行中实现超音速

📰 Aviation Today 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 固定翼 🏢 Hermeus

文章摘要

Hermeus’ Quarterhorse Mk 2.1 has achieved supersonic flight, marking the first time an unmanned aircraft has reached such speeds. The test flight demonstrated the vehicle’s ability to exceed Mach 1, validating key technologies for high-speed flight. This milestone advances Hermeus’ goal of developing reusable hypersonic aircraft for defense and commercial applications. The Quarterhorse Mk 2.1 is part of a series of progressively faster prototypes, with future iterations aiming for higher speeds and operational capabilities. The successful supersonic flight represents a significant step in unmanned aviation and hypersonic propulsion.
