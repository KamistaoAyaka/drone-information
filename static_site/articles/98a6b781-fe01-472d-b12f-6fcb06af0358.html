NATS、DroneCloud与Network Rail完成试验，解锁关键基础设施周边的无人机作业

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 NATS

文章摘要

NATS, DroneCloud, and Network Rail have successfully completed a trial aimed at enabling safe drone operations near critical infrastructure, such as railways. The trial tested a system integrating real-time drone tracking and airspace management to prevent conflicts with manned aircraft and ensure operational safety. Key outcomes include the validation of a digital handover process between drone operators and air traffic control, as well as the use of geofencing to restrict drone flights in sensitive areas. This collaboration demonstrates a viable framework for routine drone use in infrastructure inspection and maintenance, potentially improving efficiency and reducing costs while maintaining high safety standards. The results support the development of regulations for beyond visual line of sight (BVLOS) drone operations in the UK.
