DroneShield宣布获得美国联合跨机构特遣部队401合同

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 DroneShield

文章摘要

DroneShield has announced a contract with the US Joint Interagency Task Force 401, a military unit focused on counter-narcotics and transnational organized crime. The contract involves the supply of DroneShield’s counter-drone technology, specifically its DroneGun Tactical and DroneSentry systems, to enhance the task force’s capabilities against unauthorized drone threats. The agreement underscores the growing demand for drone detection and mitigation solutions in defense and security operations. DroneShield, an Australian company specializing in electronic warfare and counter-unmanned aircraft systems, expects the contract to contribute to its revenue growth and strengthen its presence in the US market. The specific financial terms were not disclosed.
