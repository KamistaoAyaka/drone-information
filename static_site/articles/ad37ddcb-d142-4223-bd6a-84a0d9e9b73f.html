DroneShield宣布获得美国联合跨机构特遣部队401合同

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 特种无人机 🏢 DroneShield

文章摘要

DroneShield has announced a contract with the US Joint Interagency Task Force 401 (JIATF 401) for the supply of counter-drone solutions. The contract involves the provision of DroneShield’s DroneGun Tactical and DroneSentry systems, which are designed to detect and neutralize unauthorized drones. This agreement underscores the growing demand for counter-unmanned aerial system (C-UAS) technology in military and interagency operations. The contract value was not disclosed, but it marks a significant expansion of DroneShield’s presence in the US defense market. The systems will be deployed to enhance security and protect against potential drone threats in sensitive operational environments.
