优步（Uber）再次尝试无人机配送

英文原标题：Uber is trying drone delivery again

📰 TechCrunch 📅 2026-06-17 🌍 国外 🛸 多旋翼 🏢 Uber

Uber将在今年年底前在美国部分市场测试使用无人机进行Uber Eats（优食）配送，这是与以色列初创公司Flytrex新合作的一部分。该合作还包括对Flytrex的一笔小额投资，不过Uber向彭博新闻社表示投资金额并不重大。

这项交易正值Uber重新涉足其曾内部开发的一些技术之际。Uber一直与Waymo（威摩）等自动驾驶汽车公司合作，在亚特兰大等城市提供自动驾驶出行服务。，并且它计划在未来几年内将电动空中出租车（electric air taxis）引入其服务中。.

优步（Uber）曾尝试使用无人机（drones）进行部分食品配送服务。 all the way back in 2019. But the company never stuck with the idea due to regulatory limits on drones and later sold its “Elevate” aviation division to the air taxi company Joby. Now the regulatory environment around new types of aircraft is loosening, and companies like Zipline are trying to make aerial delivery a part of everyday life. Flytrex also works with Uber Eats competitor DoorDash, and the startup says it has made “over 200,000 deliveries” across the U.S.
