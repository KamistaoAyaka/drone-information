Indie App Spotlight: ‘Cats Lock’ is a fun Mac app that solves cat keyboard spam

📰 Drone DJ 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

The Indie App Spotlight series highlights 'Cats Lock,' a playful and practical Mac application designed to address a common pet owner problem: cats walking on keyboards and causing unintended keystrokes. This lightweight app provides a simple solution by locking the keyboard when a cat is detected nearby, preventing accidental spam or disruptive inputs. Using the Mac's built-in camera or proximity sensors, Cats Lock intelligently identifies feline presence and temporarily disables keyboard functionality until the cat moves away. The app is praised for its fun, user-friendly interface and effectiveness, offering pet owners peace of mind while working. It is an indie creation that combines humor with utility, making it a standout tool for cat lovers who use Macs. Overall, Cats Lock solves a niche but relatable issue with a clever, non-intrusive approach.
