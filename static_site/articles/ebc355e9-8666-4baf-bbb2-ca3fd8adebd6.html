Eurosatory 2026 – LACROIX、Bertin、MC2 合作保护装甲车辆免受无人机（drone）攻击

📰 Unmanned Airspace 📅 2026-06-16 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 LACROIX, Bertin, MC2

文章摘要

At Eurosatory 2026, LACROIX, Bertin, and MC2 announced a collaboration to develop protection systems for armored vehicles against drone attacks. The partnership combines LACROIX’s expertise in countermeasure systems, Bertin’s capabilities in detection and identification technologies, and MC2’s specialization in electronic warfare and cyber defense. Their joint effort aims to create integrated solutions that detect, track, and neutralize hostile drones, addressing the growing threat of unmanned aerial systems on the battlefield. The collaboration focuses on enhancing vehicle survivability through layered defense mechanisms, including soft-kill and hard-kill options. This initiative reflects the increasing need for adaptive counter-drone technologies in modern military operations.
