Starship Flight 12 inches closer to launch with booster and ship static fires

📰 Drone DJ 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

SpaceX's Starship program has taken a significant step forward with the successful completion of static fire tests for both the Super Heavy booster and the Starship upper stage, bringing Flight 12 closer to launch. During these tests, the booster's 33 Raptor engines and the ship's six engines were briefly ignited while the vehicles remained grounded, verifying their performance and readiness. This milestone follows a series of preparations, including vehicle stacking and pre-launch checks at the Starbase facility in Texas. The static fires are critical for validating engine functionality, propellant systems, and structural integrity before the full integrated flight test. While an exact launch date has not been announced, these tests indicate that SpaceX is progressing toward the next orbital attempt, aiming to demonstrate key capabilities such as stage separation, payload deployment, and controlled reentry. The company continues to iterate on design improvements based on previous flight data, with the goal of advancing Starship's development for future missions to the Moon, Mars, and beyond.
