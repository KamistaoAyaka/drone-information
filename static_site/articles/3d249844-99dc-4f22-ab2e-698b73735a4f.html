摩托罗拉解决方案公司（Motorola Solutions）将收购反无人机系统行业领导者D-Fend Solutions（D-Fend解决方案公司）

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 其他无人机 🏢 Motorola Solutions

文章摘要

Motorola Solutions has announced its acquisition of D-Fend Solutions, a leading provider of counter-drone technology. D-Fend specializes in radio frequency-based, non-kinetic drone mitigation systems, which detect and safely disable unauthorized drones without causing physical destruction. This acquisition aims to enhance Motorola Solutions’ existing security and public safety portfolio by integrating advanced drone detection and response capabilities. The deal is expected to address growing threats from rogue drones in critical infrastructure, airports, and public events. Key terms of the transaction were not disclosed. The acquisition is subject to regulatory approvals and is anticipated to close in the coming months.
