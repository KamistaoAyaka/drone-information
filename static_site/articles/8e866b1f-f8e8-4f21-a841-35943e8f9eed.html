Hermeus的Quarterhorse Mk 2.1飞机在首次无人飞行中实现超音速

📰 Aviation Today 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 固定翼 🏢 Hermeus

文章摘要

Hermeus’ Quarterhorse Mk 2.1 has become the first unmanned aircraft to achieve supersonic flight. The milestone was reached during a test flight where the aircraft exceeded Mach 1, marking a significant advancement in high-speed unmanned aviation. The Quarterhorse Mk 2.1 is part of Hermeus’ development program for a reusable hypersonic aircraft, designed to eventually carry passengers at speeds over Mach 5. This flight validates the company’s engine technology and airframe design, moving closer to operational hypersonic travel. The successful supersonic test demonstrates progress in autonomous flight systems and thermal management at high speeds.
