NATS, DroneCloud and Network Rail complete trial to unlock drone operations around critical infrastructure

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外

文章摘要

NATS, DroneCloud, and Network Rail have completed a trial aimed at enabling safe drone operations near critical infrastructure, such as railways and airports. The trial tested a system integrating real-time drone tracking and airspace management to prevent conflicts with manned aircraft and ensure operational safety. Key outcomes include successful demonstration of geofencing, automated authorization, and data sharing between drone operators and air traffic control. This initiative addresses regulatory and safety challenges, potentially allowing drones to be used for infrastructure inspection and maintenance without disrupting existing aviation operations. The results support the development of scalable, secure drone integration into complex airspace environments.
