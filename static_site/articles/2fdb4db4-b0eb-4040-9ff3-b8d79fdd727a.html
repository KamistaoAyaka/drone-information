MacGyver、Squishy Tech、YGGY Aviation 等受邀参加 DARPA 升力挑战赛（DARPA Lift Challenge）

📰 Aviation Today 📅 2026-06-17 🌍 国外 🛸 垂直起降

文章摘要

DARPA has invited teams including MacGyver, Squishy Tech, and YGGY Aviation to participate in its Lift Challenge. The competition aims to advance vertical takeoff and landing (VTOL) technology, focusing on innovative designs that do not require traditional runways. Participants will demonstrate aircraft capable of carrying useful payloads while achieving efficient hover and forward flight. The challenge seeks to accelerate development of practical, high-performance VTOL systems for military and civilian applications. Key objectives include improving aerodynamic efficiency, reducing noise, and enhancing operational flexibility. The selected teams represent diverse approaches to solving these engineering problems, with evaluations based on flight performance metrics and technical feasibility.
