Blue Origin looking to complete booster reuse on its third mission, tomorrow

📰 Drone DJ 📅 2026-05-17 🌍 国外

文章摘要

Blue Origin is preparing for its third mission, scheduled for tomorrow, with a key focus on achieving booster reuse. The company aims to demonstrate the reusability of its rocket booster, a critical step toward reducing launch costs and increasing flight frequency. This mission follows two previous successful flights, and Blue Origin seeks to land the booster intact after launch, allowing it to be refurbished and flown again. The attempt underscores the company's commitment to developing reusable launch technology, similar to SpaceX's Falcon 9 program. If successful, this would mark a significant milestone for Blue Origin's New Shepard or New Glenn vehicle, depending on the mission specifics. The launch is expected to take place from Blue Origin's facility in West Texas, with weather and technical checks ongoing. The outcome will provide valuable data for future missions and the broader commercial space industry.
