NATS、DroneCloud和Network Rail完成试验，以解锁关键基础设施周边的无人机运行

📰 sUAS News 📅 2026-06-08 🌍 国外 🛸 多旋翼

文章摘要

NATS, DroneCloud, and Network Rail have successfully completed a trial aimed at enabling safe drone operations near critical infrastructure, such as railways. The trial tested a system integrating drone traffic management with existing air traffic control to ensure drones can operate without interfering with manned aircraft or sensitive sites. Key outcomes include demonstrating real-time data sharing between DroneCloud’s platform and NATS’ air traffic systems, allowing for dynamic airspace management. The collaboration also validated procedures for drone flights in controlled zones, highlighting potential for improved infrastructure inspection and maintenance efficiency. This trial represents a step toward integrating drones into national airspace while maintaining safety and security around critical assets.
